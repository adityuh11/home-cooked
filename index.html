Tofu Lettuce Wraps

Method

6 steps
  1. Soak the shiitakes in hot water for about 15 minutes. Squeeze dry and mince finely.
  2. Mix hoisin, peanut butter, rice vinegar, chili sauce, sugar, soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic and ginger. Keep the finishing water aside.
  3. Cut tofu into very small cubes. Heat the cooking oil in a good nonstick pan and brown the tofu, letting it sit before tossing so it develops crisp edges.
  4. Add mushrooms, water chestnuts and the pale part of the green onion. Cook about 1 minute.
  5. Add half the sauce and toss for about 30 seconds. Stir in the green onion greens.
  6. Thin the remaining sauce with the finishing water. Spoon the filling into cold lettuce leaves, finish with peanuts and drizzle with sauce.

Method

6 steps
  1. Press the tofu for 10–15 minutes. Tear into rough 3–4 cm pieces rather than neat cubes for more craggy surface area.
  2. Toss with cornstarch, salt, garlic powder and oil until every piece is lightly dusted.
  3. Air fry at 205°C / 400°F for 12 minutes. Shake, then cook another 5–8 minutes until deeply crisp.
  4. Whisk the sauce ingredients together.
  5. Simmer the sauce in a pan for 30–60 seconds, just until glossy and thick enough to cling.
  6. Turn off the heat, add the crisp tofu and toss briefly. Serve immediately so the crust stays crisp.
Kitchen note: Sauce only after the tofu is fully crisp. Torn edges + a dry starch coating are what give this version its airy-crunchy shell.

Method

6 steps
  1. Mix tofu, water, soy sauce, neutral oil and brown sugar. Separately mix vital wheat gluten, garlic powder, onion powder and salt. Add wet to dry and stir only until combined — do not knead. Rest 15–20 minutes.
  2. Shape into a thick patty, wrap loosely in foil and steam 25–30 minutes. Cool 5–10 minutes, then cut into bite-size pieces.
  3. Toss with cornstarch and optional baking powder. Air fry at 190°C / 375°F for 10–14 minutes with a light oil spray, shaking halfway. Or pan-fry until golden.
  4. Bloom the chili flakes briefly in a dry pan or in whatever amount of oil you enter above.
  5. Add soy sauce, brown sugar, vinegar, hoisin, water, cornstarch, garlic powder, ginger powder and optional ketchup. Cook 30–60 seconds until glossy and thick.
  6. Toss the crisp seitan in the sauce until coated. Add bell pepper, onion or carrot if you want, and serve over rice.
Kitchen note: Optional ingredients start at the amount shown. Set sesame oil to 0 g to skip it, or type 2 g ketchup / ~4.5 g sauce oil if you use those upgrades.

Method

6 steps

Method

6 steps
  1. Heat the oven to 205°C / 400°F. Press the tofu for 10–15 minutes and cut into 2–3 cm cubes.
  2. Cut the sweet potatoes into roughly 3 cm chunks. Toss them with about two-thirds of the olive oil and half the spice mixture.
  3. Toss the tofu separately with the remaining oil and remaining spices.
  4. Spread everything on a sheet pan and roast 15 minutes. Flip, then roast another 10–15 minutes until browned and tender.
  5. Mix vegan mayo, lemon juice, cilantro, agave and the extra smoked paprika. Season to taste.
  6. Serve the roasted tofu and sweet potatoes with the aioli drizzled over or alongside for dipping.
Kitchen note: The cinnamon should stay subtle. It rounds out the cumin, turmeric and paprika instead of making the dish taste sweet.

  • spinach or napa cabbage
  • yellow / white onionoptional scallion substitute; try 80–100 g
  • Sauce

Live macros. Change any ingredient amount and the nutrition updates with it. Different brands can vary slightly.

Method

6 steps
  1. Bring 3–4 liters of unsalted water to a rolling boil. Fully submerge the dry dangmyeon and cook 6–7 minutes. Drain, rinse under cold water 15–20 seconds and cut into 3–4 cm pieces.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, garlic and rice vinegar.
  3. Heat about one-third of the neutral oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high. Scramble the eggs softly, then remove and set aside.
  4. Add another third of the oil. Stir-fry carrots and mushrooms 2–3 minutes. If using onion, cook it here. Add spinach or cabbage just until wilted.
  5. Add the noodles and cooked brown rice. Spread them out and let them sit against the hot pan for 20–30 seconds before stirring; repeat once for more fried-rice texture.
  6. Pour in the sauce, toss until glossy, then fold the egg back in. Finish with sesame seeds. No scallions needed.
Kitchen note: If you skip scallions entirely, the dish is still structurally fine. If you want the aromatic lift, enter 80–100 g onion above and cook it with the carrots + mushrooms.
